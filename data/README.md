scans, a use case for each, an edge-case file and example images. **The CSVs are copied verbatim
into `data/course/`; the scans are not stored here** (≈ 100 MB) — download the folder and open the

## Format notes

* Coordinates are voxel indices `M[x][y][z]`. The first column of the course format is the
  number of points (3 or 4) and must match the points given; the points are listed `a, b, c`
  or `a1, b, a2, c` (rail by rail). Plain rows of 9 or 12 numbers are also accepted; spaces and
  exponents (`1.59e2`) are fine.
* All NRRD files use the attached-data form (`.nrrd`); detached headers (`.nhdr` + raw file)
  are supported by the reader and covered by a test, so Slicer exports in either form work.
  `space directions` are read for the app's display (patient axes, millimetres).
* Outputs of the dataset runs are in `results/` and discussed in `docs/EXPERIMENT.md`.
