## Format notes

* Coordinates are voxel indices `M[x][y][z]`; the first column of the course format is the
  number of points (3 or 4); plain rows of 9 or 12 numbers are also accepted.
* All NRRD files use the attached-data form (`.nrrd`); detached headers (`.nhdr` + raw file)
  are supported by the reader and covered by a test, so Slicer exports in either form work.
* Outputs of the dataset runs are in `results/` and discussed in `docs/EXPERIMENT.md`.
